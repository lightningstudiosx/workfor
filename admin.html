How to run it
  • Hire someone: open their application on GitHub, add the label hired, and leave a comment to welcome them. (GitHub emails them about your comment.)
  • Turn someone down: leave a kind comment and close the issue.
  • Check the weekly programs: look at the "programs" tab. Anyone hired who hasn't handed one in this week is listed under "missing this week".
  • GitHub emails you whenever a new application or program arrives, as long as you're watching the repo.
