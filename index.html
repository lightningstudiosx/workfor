The job

Make one program every week and hand it in. That's the whole job.

It can be any size and do anything at all: a game, a calculator, a website, a bot, an animation, or a program that prints "hi". If it runs, it counts.

Requirements

  • Hand in 1 program a week, no matter the size or what it does.
  • Any programming language is fine (Scratch counts).
  • It has to be something you made yourself.
  • A free GitHub account. You use it to apply and to hand in your programs.
  • You must be 13 or older, because that's GitHub's rule.

Pay and benefits

  • Pay is −$0 a week. That's zero dollars. This job is unpaid, and it always will be.
  • Good programs might be featured on kai.fun, with your name on them.
  • You get a lot better at programming. One program a week adds up to 52 a year.

The fine print

This is a volunteer, just-for-fun position. There is no money, no contract and no boss who can make you do anything. You can stop whenever you like, just by not handing in a program. kai.fun will never ask you for money, passwords or personal details beyond what's on this form.

Apply

When you press "Send application", GitHub opens with your application filled in. Press Create there to send it to the kai.fun admin. Applications are posted on GitHub, where anyone can see them, so only write what you're happy to share.
